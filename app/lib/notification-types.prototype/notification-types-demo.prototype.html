PROTOTYPENotification Types: three ways to store them

Make things happen on Cadence with the buttons on the left, then look at anyone's Notifications. Each Notification is raised once, for the same Recipients, and then stored three different ways: A keeps only ids and looks everything up when shown, B keeps the finished sentence, C (the one we chose) keeps a snapshot of the facts and writes the sentence when shown. Yellow means "this changed since your last click". Click a Notification to mark it read.
